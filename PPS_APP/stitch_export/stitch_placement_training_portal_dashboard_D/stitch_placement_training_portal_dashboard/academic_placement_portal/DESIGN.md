## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an academic, structured, and modern vocational environment engineered for university placement tracks, career portfolios, and pedagogical assessments. It replaces traditional literary ornamentation with an uncluttered, modern sans-serif aesthetic, combining structural intellectual rigor with human usability.

### Core Character
- **Clean & Academic**: Focuses on structured data presentation, clear information hierarchies, and quiet visual restraint. It avoids tech-startup exuberance, floating glass effects, and decorative noise.
- **Modern Sans-Serif Clarity**: Uses an intentional dual-sans pairing—Manrope for authoritative titles and navigational anchors, paired with Inter for high-density ledgers, student records, and explanatory text.
- **Calm & Humane**: Low-stimulus surfaces, natural paper-inspired warm whites, and balanced contrasts reduce cognitive fatigue during high-stakes placement reviews, resume scoring, and interview scheduling.

## Colors

The color architecture remains centered on authentic paper tones, muted mist blues, and subdued status indicators, completely devoid of high-saturation neon highlights.

### Color Tiers & Application
- **Primary Accent (`#4A6B82`)**: The foundational mist-blue tone used for interactive buttons, progress indicators, focused field boundaries, and selected tab anchors. Transitions to `#3B5A70` on hover.
- **Backgrounds & Canvases (`#F8F9FD` / `#FBFBFA`)**: Clean, low-glare surface tints providing sustained legibility across lengthy reading and analytical tasks.
- **Surfaces & Containers (`#FFFFFF` / `#F2F3F7` / `#ECEEF2`)**: Clear planar backgrounds for modular cards, metric ledgers, and tabular data containers.
- **Typographic Charcoal (`#1A1F24`)**: Deep, low-fatigue charcoal for primary headings and body text, supported by `#42474C` for secondary metadata and subtitles.
- **System Status Indicators**:
  - *Success / Validated*: Muted Sage (`#4E7D63`) paired with soft sage tint (`#EBF3EE`).
  - *Warning / Deadline Pending*: Soft Amber (`#A87438`) paired with ivory-amber tint (`#FBF4EA`).
  - *Alert / Critical / Incomplete*: Soft Crimson (`#BA1A1A`) paired with gentle rose tint (`#FFDAD6`).

## Typography

The typography system is constructed strictly with a modern, academic, clean sans-serif pairing. All serif styles (including Newsreader) are deprecated.

### Role Allocation
- **Manrope (Display, Headings, Navigation, Buttons, Badges, Metrics)**:
  - Display and major section titles utilize `Manrope Bold` (700) with slight negative tracking to establish clear architectural grounding.
  - Section subheadings, card titles, and navigation links utilize `Manrope Semibold` (600).
  - Badges, status tokens, button labels, and small interface metadata utilize `Manrope Medium` (500) and `Semibold` (600).
  - Quantitative scores and metric statistics leverage `Manrope Bold` (700) with tabular figure alignment.
- **Inter (Body Text, Form Inputs, Tables, Descriptions, Helper Text)**:
  - Explanatory paragraphs, feedback documentation, and contextual notes utilize `Inter Regular` (400) for uncompromised screen readability.
  - Form field inputs, dropdown choices, and table cells use `Inter Regular` (400) and `Inter Medium` (500).
  - Dates, small captions, helper guidelines, and citation footnotes use `Inter Regular` (400) in `body-sm`.

## Layout & Spacing

The portal adheres to a modular column-based layout engineered to cleanly balance dense data grids, student review files, and analytical assessment metrics.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column grid capped at 1320px max content width. Outer margins set to `3rem`, column gutters fixed to `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with fluid `2rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px)**: 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` gutters. Analytical tables reflow into discrete border-bounded cards.

### Spatial Hierarchy
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled inline components, badge padding, and label-input groupings.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, metric cell insets, and list spacing.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) and `space-xl` (40px) to delineate independent functional modules and section dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through minimal tonal layering and thin structural rules, eschewing dramatic drop shadows and heavy skeuomorphic effects.

### Layering Rules
- **Base Canvas**: Neutral `#F8F9FD` or warm `#FBFBFA` surface.
- **Resting Containers (Cards, Ledgers, Dossiers)**: White background (`#FFFFFF`) defined by a crisp, low-contrast 1px border (`#E6E4DF` or `#C2C7CD`). Elevation shadows are either omitted entirely or restricted to an ultra-subtle contact shadow: `0 1px 2px rgba(25, 28, 31, 0.04)`.
- **Overlays (Modals, Active Popovers, Filter Menus)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` supported by an intentional dual-stage shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(25, 28, 31, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(25, 28, 31, 0.04)` and a 1px `#E6E4DF` border.
- **Recessed Areas**: Inactive tabs, table headers, and secondary metric trays use `#F2F3F7` or `#ECEEF2` without inset shadows.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a clean, architectural shape language (`roundedness: 1`). Elements favor slight, purposeful corner rounding to maintain a modern, official, and academic register without appearing playful or excessively bubbly.

### Component Radii
- **Default (Inputs, Buttons, Cards, Modals)**: `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px).
- **Interactive Badges & Status Indicators**: `0.25rem` (4px) to sustain crisp boundary definitions.
- **Avatar & Notification Pips**: `9999px` strictly for user identity chips and count badges.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Buttons**: Solid mist-blue (`#4A6B82`) fill, white (`#FFFFFF`) text, styled in `Manrope Semibold` (`label-md`). 4px corner radius. On hover: `#3B5A70`. Focus ring: 2px offset in `#4A6B82`.
- **Secondary Buttons**: White (`#FFFFFF`) fill with a 1px `#E6E4DF` border, `#1A1F24` text in `Manrope Semibold`. On hover: surface transitions to `#F2F3F7`.
- **Ghost / Text Actions**: Transparent fill, `#4A6B82` text in `Manrope Semibold`, with an underline rendered on hover.

### Badges, Status Chips, & Scores
- Always set in `Manrope Medium` or `Semibold` (`label-sm`), uppercase or title case.
- **Success / Validated**: `#EBF3EE` background, `#4E7D63` label, 1px `#4E7D63` border at 20% opacity.
- **Deadline Warning / Review**: `#FBF4EA` background, `#A87438` label, 1px `#A87438` border at 20% opacity.
- **Alert / Remediation Required**: `#FFDAD6` background, `#BA1A1A` label, 1px `#BA1A1A` border at 20% opacity.
- **Metric Containers**: Highlight scores rendered in `metric-display` (`Manrope Bold`, 2rem) with tabular figures, paired with descriptive `Inter` subtitles (`body-sm`).

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px `#E6E4DF` boundary. `0.25rem` corner radius.
- Value text rendered in `Inter Regular` (`body-md`, `#1A1F24`); placeholder in `#72787D`.
- Form labels set above the field in `Manrope Semibold` (`label-sm`, `#1A1F24`), with helper instructions below in `Inter Regular` (`body-sm`, `#42474C`).
- Focused state highlights the border with `#4A6B82` and applies a 2px soft mist ring.

### Data Tables & Student Registers
- Table headers use surface `#F2F3F7` with column identifiers in `Manrope Semibold` (`label-sm`, `#42474C`).
- Data rows utilize clean 1px horizontal borders in `#E6E4DF`. Alternating zebra stripes are disallowed.
- Table body cells render text and numerical metadata in `Inter Regular` and `Medium` (`body-md` / `body-sm`).
- Hover row state shifts smoothly to `#F8F9FD`.

### Cards & Evaluation Modules
- Pure `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px `#E6E4DF` boundary line.
- Masthead headers use `Manrope Semibold` (`headline-sm` or `title-md`).
- Qualitative evaluations, assessment comments, and advisor notes render in `Inter Regular` (`body-md`).
